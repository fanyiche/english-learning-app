## unit: student-u4 | 学术写作 | B1
w: thesis | 毕业论文；论点 | Her thesis is about online learning. | 她的论文是关于在线学习的。
w: argument | 论点；论证 | Your argument needs more support. | 你的论点需要更多支撑。
w: source | 资料来源 | Use at least five reliable sources. | 至少使用五个可靠的资料来源。
w: cite | 引用（并注明出处） | Always cite the author and the year. | 一定要注明作者和年份。
w: summary | 摘要；总结 | Write a short summary of the article. | 写一段文章的简短摘要。
w: paragraph | 段落 | Each paragraph should have one main idea. | 每个段落应该只有一个中心思想。
w: conclusion | 结论 | The conclusion repeats the main points. | 结论重申了要点。
w: introduction | 引言；开头 | A good introduction gets attention. | 好的开头能吸引注意。
g: 表达观点 | I believe / I think + 句子 | 用 I believe 引出自己的观点，比 I think 更正式。 | I believe online classes are useful. | 我认为网课很有用。 | 选择最正式的表达观点 | I believe this is true. / I want this is true. / I am believe this is true. | I believe this is true.
g: 被动语态描述研究 | It is + 过去分词 + that… | 学术写作常用被动语态让表达更客观。 | It is known that sleep helps memory. | 众所周知，睡眠有助于记忆。 | 选择正确的学术表达 | It knows that exercise helps. / It is known that exercise helps. / It is know that exercise helps. | It is known that exercise helps.
g: 转折 | However, … | However 表示转折，通常放在句首并加逗号。 | The method is cheap. However, it is slow. | 这种方法很便宜，但是很慢。 | 选择正确的转折句 | The data is useful, however it is old. / The data is useful. However, it is old. / The data is useful. However it, is old. | The data is useful. However, it is old.
d: Tutor | Your argument is interesting, but your sources are weak. | 你的论点很有意思，但资料来源不够有力。
d: Student | Should I add more recent studies? | 我应该加一些更新的研究吗？
d: Tutor | Yes, and remember to cite every source. | 对，记得每个来源都要注明出处。
d: Student | Got it. I will also rewrite the conclusion. | 明白了，我也会重写结论。

## unit: lawyer-u3 | 合同条款 | B1

w: breach | 违约；违反 | A late payment is a breach of contract. | 逾期付款属于违约。
w: party | （合同）一方 | Each party must sign the agreement. | 每一方都必须签署协议。
w: obligation | 义务 | The seller has an obligation to deliver on time. | 卖方有按时交货的义务。

w: renew | 续签；续期 | We want to renew the contract for one year. | 我们想把合同续签一年。

g: 义务与禁止 | shall / shall not + 动词原形 | 合同中 shall 表示“应当”，shall not 表示“不得”。 | The buyer shall pay within 30 days. | 买方应在 30 日内付款。 | 选择正确的合同表达 | The tenant shall not sublet the flat. / The tenant shall not sublets the flat. / The tenant not shall sublet the flat. | The tenant shall not sublet the flat.

g: 截止时间 | no later than + 时间 | no later than 表示“不迟于”，比 before 更严格。 | Notice must be given no later than June 1. | 通知须不迟于 6 月 1 日发出。 | 补全：Payment is due no ___ than Friday. | later / late / more | later
